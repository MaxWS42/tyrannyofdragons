We then tracked the cultists into the Mere of Dead Men. Having come upon a small camp with canoes, we met [[Tyranny of Dragons/People#^70b780\|Snapjaw]] and learned about the Cult's occupation of the Castle with its bullywug allies, and that the lizardfolk were being paid for their labour in metal tools.
Once within Castle Naerytar ([ground](/img/user/Tyranny%20of%20Dragons/Images/Castle%20Naerytar/Castle%20Naerytar%20Ground%20Level.jpg), [level 2](/img/user/Tyranny%20of%20Dragons/Images/Castle%20Naerytar/Castle%20Naerytar%20Level%202.jpg), [level 3](/img/user/Tyranny%20of%20Dragons/Images/Castle%20Naerytar/Castle%20Naerytar%20Level%203.jpg)), we incited a lizardfolk rebellion (with Snapjaw's help) to overthrow the tyrannous cult and killed Rezmir (for the first time...). We also encountered and killed [[Tyranny of Dragons/People#^0316fe\|Jos]] *?* in a tower.

We also learnt the progression of cult ranks, that is, from dragonclaw, dragonwing, dragonfang, dragonsoul to wyrmspeaker.

In Castle Naerytar, we then proceeded through an [underground area](/img/user/Tyranny%20of%20Dragons/Images/Castle%20Naerytar/Castle%20Naerytar%20Dungeon.jpg) and found a portal, that took us to a [[Tyranny of Dragons/Events/6 Hunting Lodge\|hunting lodge]] in the Graypeak Mountains.
